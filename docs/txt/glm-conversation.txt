   n² + 2n + 1 = (n + 1)²

It matches the standard identity for the square of a binomial:

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Here, take a = n and b = 1:
   a² = n²
   2ab = 2·n·1 = 2n
   b² = 1² = 1

   (n + 1)² = n² + 2n + 1
